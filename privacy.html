Goldy's Bagels

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Goldy's Bagels ("we", "us") operates a preorder app for pickup at Blue Sky Organic Farms in Litchfield Park, Arizona. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

The short version: we collect only what we need to bake your order and tell you when it's ready. We never sell your personal information, and we never see your full card number.

Information we collect

  • Order details — your name, phone number, email address, chosen pickup date, and what you ordered.
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe. Card numbers are entered directly into Stripe's secure fields and go straight to Stripe; we never receive, store, or have access to your full card number, expiry, or CVV.
  • Push notification token — a device token Apple issues so we can tell you when your bagels go in the oven and when they're ready for pickup.
  • Sign in with Apple — if you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email address Apple provides. You can choose to hide your email with Apple's private relay.

How we use it

  • To bake, fulfil, and hand over your order at the farm.
  • To contact you about your order — including changes, delays, and pickup readiness.
  • To send order-status push notifications, if you've allowed them.
  • To keep basic business records for accounting and tax purposes.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — push notification delivery (APNs) and Sign in with Apple.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don't build advertising profiles.

Retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to run the business and meet accounting and tax obligations. You can ask us to delete your contact details at any time — see below.

Your choices and rights

  • Push notifications can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings › Notifications › Goldy's Bagels. Turning them off won't affect your orders.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the CCPA. We honour them all the same.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We protect the order information we hold with access controls and encrypted connections in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be called out in the app.
