GOLDY'S BAGELS

Brand Identity Guide

01 — Brand Essence

Why We Exist

"Growing up on the East Coast, bagels were everywhere. When our family moved to Arizona, it was a bagel desert — so we made our own."

Goldy's is the taste of home, carried 2,000 miles across the country and recreated from scratch in a family kitchen. Every bagel is a love letter to East Coast tradition, made with Arizona grit.

04 — Logo

Logo Mark

The bagel mark works on light and dark backgrounds. On dark backgrounds, the text inverts to Cream Dough with the subtitle in Golden Crust. The mark should never be stretched, rotated, or recolored outside the brand palette.

05 — Voice & Tone

How We Talk

Goldy's voice is warm, proud, and unpretentious. We speak like a family sharing something they love — not like a brand selling something. Short, rhythmic, real.

We Say

"Hand-rolled. Cold-fermented. Stone-baked. Ready to shmear."

"The bagel desert is over."

"We learned to make our own — step by step."

We Don't Say

"Artisanal handcrafted gourmet bagels made with premium ingredients."

"Elevate your breakfast experience."

"The finest bagels in the greater Phoenix metropolitan area."

Tone Keywords

Warm · Proud · Familial · Honest · Rhythmic · Specific

Sample Headline

"East Coast tradition.
Arizona grit.
One hell of a bagel."

06 — Photography Direction

Visual Style

Lighting

Natural, warm. Golden hour or soft morning light. No harsh studio flash. Think farmers market at 7am.

Subject

Hands rolling dough, bagels on stone, steam rising, shmear being spread, stacked bagels at the stand. Process over product.

Color

Warm tones dominant. Desaturate blues and cool tones slightly. Let the golden crust and cream dough tones lead.

Mood

Intimate, handmade, real. No stock-photo perfection. Slightly rustic. Flour dust on the counter is a feature, not a flaw.

People

Family members, customers at the market, hands (always hands). Faces are welcome but hands tell the story better.

Avoid

Overhead flat-lay spreads, generic coffee-shop shots, anything that looks like it was photographed in a studio.
