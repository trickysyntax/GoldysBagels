Our Story

From the East Coast to a bagel desert.

Bagels were everywhere on the east coast — and they were good. Growing up Jewish, they were woven into the rhythm of the week. Every Sunday morning, every family gathering, every quick bite on the way to school. It wasn't just food. It was home.

Then, in the 1990s, our whole family picked up our roots and moved to Arizona. It wasn't just a literal desert — it was a bagel desert. What passed for a bagel out here was a roll with a hole in it. No chew. No crust. No soul.

So we learned to make our own. Step by step. What started as homesickness became a craft — decades of perfecting the old way, until friends and neighbors started asking when we were going to sell them.

We're a multigenerational family of ten, spread across three houses right here in Verrado. The adults all have day jobs and kids to chase after. This isn't a bakery — it's a labor of love. We make bagels that are too good not to share with our community. The bagel desert is over.

The Craft

No shortcuts. Just the old way.

Every Goldy's bagel is hand-rolled and cold-fermented before it ever sees the oven. Here's what goes into each one.

01

The Dough

High-gluten USA wheat flour, kosher salt, dark brown sugar, yeast, water, and oil. Simple ingredients, exacting ratios.

02

Hand-Rolled

Each bagel is shaped by hand the old way — no machines, no extruders. Every one is a little different, and that's the point.

03

Cold Ferment

The shaped dough rests in the cold. That slow ferment builds the flavor, the chew, and the character.

04

Boil & Bake

We shock the proofed dough in boiling malted water, then bake on stone. Ready to shmear in minutes.
